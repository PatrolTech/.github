## Before handing over

- [ ] The location is specific enough for the next shift to find it.
- [ ] The note separates observations from assumptions.
- [ ] Photo references and checkpoint references can be matched to the incident.
- [ ] Completed actions are separate from requested actions.
- [ ] An open issue has a next step and a named team or role.
- [ ] The handover states whether acknowledgement is still outstanding.
- [ ] If recorded offline, visit time and synchronization time are understood separately.

### Fictional example

**Demo Site A, loading-bay door, 21:10 local time.** The door was open when visited. A photo was attached. The duty supervisor was notified at 21:14. Next step: the facilities team checks the latch. Status: open; acknowledgement pending. This example describes an observation and follow-up, not a conclusion about why the door was open.

## Plantilla en español

Referencia · Sitio y punto de control · Fecha, hora y zona horaria · Autor o referencia interna · Hechos observados · Fotos o evidencias relacionadas · Actuación realizada · Persona/equipo avisado y hora · Responsable del siguiente paso · Estado · Revisión o cierre.

Antes del relevo: identifica el lugar, separa hechos y suposiciones, relaciona las evidencias, distingue lo realizado de lo solicitado y deja claro quién continúa la gestión. Si el registro se capturó sin conexión, distingue la hora del hecho de la hora de sincronización.
