## What is PatrolTech?

**PatrolTech is guard tour and security patrol software for security companies, facilities teams and property managers.** Developed by **Ingenieros Web SL** in Madrid, Spain, it brings checkpoint recording in the field and patrol review in a management dashboard into one workflow.

Officers record **QR/NFC checkpoint visits** and incidents with photos and notes. They can continue recording while offline; records synchronize when connectivity returns. Managers organize sites and routes, review synchronized activity and prepare reports.

The product was founded in **2024**. PatrolTech is available in **English, Spanish and French**.

## From the site to the supervisor

| In the field | In the dashboard |
| --- | --- |
| Record QR/NFC checkpoint visits | Configure sites, checkpoints and routes |
| Keep recording without continuous connectivity | Review patrol records after synchronization |
| Report incidents with photos and notes | Review incidents and prepare reports |

**A practical example:** an officer records a checkpoint visit in a basement without mobile coverage. The record stays on the device and synchronizes after reconnection. The visit time and synchronization time describe different events.
